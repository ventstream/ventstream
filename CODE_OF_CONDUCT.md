# Code of Conduct

VentStream contributors must communicate professionally and focus discussion
on the technical work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publication of
private information, and personal attacks are not accepted in project spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when this policy is violated.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Reports
will be handled confidentially and reviewed without retaliation against a
good-faith reporter.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issue trackers, pull requests,
community channels, and project events.
